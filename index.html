I owned a tooling problem from the shop floor to working software: found the bottleneck, automated the design work, then turned the script into a tool other engineers could actually use.

Found the bottleneck

Mapped the cylinder-block line (5 processes) and pinned fine boring as the bottleneck. Proposed automating boring-bar design across 20+ body variants and 7+ cartridge types.

Automated the CAD

VB.NET app on the Solid Edge COM API: parametric boring bars with HSK 63 shanks, pockets, angled planes and tapped holes, plus assembly mating with planar and axial constraints.

Killed manual drafting

PMI-based dimensioning and drawing sheets generated straight from the assembly, so every output looks the same.

Made it a product

Windows Forms GUI for non-technical users, a REST API layer, a PostgreSQL store for job history and variant versions, and unit and integration tests.
